    What the stress number means
    1. Every 10 s the suit takes the last 60 s: heart rate and its variability (RMSSD, SDNN, pNN50), skin conductance level, trend and responses.
    2. Each is compared with your own 3-minute rest calibration.
    3. A small random forest (100 trees) trained on WESAD (15 people, a lab stress test) gives P(stress); the suit shows the average of the last 30 s and says “stressed” at 50 % or more.
    4. While you move it pauses — walking changes heart rate and sweating too.
    5. A research prototype: it is not a medical device and does not diagnose stress or anxiety.
    Install as an app on your phone
    1. Open this page once in Chrome on the phone (it is saved for offline use).
    2. Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app.
    3. Open SereniSuit BT from the icon and tap Connect via Bluetooth.
    4. No need to join the suit's Wi-Fi — Bluetooth is enough.
